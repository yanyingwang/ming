6.13.3 㞢🔗ℹ

㞢
ideograph

  connotation : apply
  originates from : 㞢
  originally means : to go
Imagines 一 as the first element(the procedure), and 凵 as the pathesis, and 丨 as an arrow, which moves the 一 to the upper position. Since we rotates 㞢 90 degrees for writing, we are now imaging the moving from left to right not below to upper.

Example:
> (㞢 􏼃 '("a" "b" "c"))

"abc"
